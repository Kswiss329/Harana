Our Heritage

Inspired by the warmth of Filipino home cooking and the richness of our archipelago’s flavors.

Harana & Co. was born from a simple belief: that the best meals connect us to where we come from. Our chefs bring time-honored recipes—from the smoky grills of Bacolod to the coconut groves of Bicol—and rebalance them for today’s health-conscious tables. We source local, honor tradition, and never compromise on taste.
